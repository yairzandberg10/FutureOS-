A single-line text input; used for rename, search, and the input dialog.

```jsx
<TextField value="מסמך חדש" focused />
<TextField placeholder="שם הקובץ" />
```

Text is right-aligned and the caret sits at its left edge (the RTL end of the run). There is no label above and no helper text below — the dialog title or the row does that job. No underline variant exists; the field is always the filled 8% shape.
