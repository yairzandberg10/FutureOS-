A circular 36dp icon button; used in the top bar and nowhere else in FutureOS.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="arrow_forward" focused />
<IconButton icon="more_vert" />
```

In RTL the back button sits at the **right** end of the bar and its arrow points right (`"arrow_forward"`, the mirrored form of `Icons.AutoMirrored.Rounded.ArrowBack`); the overflow button sits at the left. The idle background is 8% text, focus is 30% accent — there is no border and no scale.
