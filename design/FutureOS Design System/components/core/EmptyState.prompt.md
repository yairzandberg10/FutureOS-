Fills the content area of a screen that has nothing in it; sits below the `TopBar`, never replacing it.

```jsx
<EmptyState icon="person" title="אין אנשי קשר" subtitle="לחץ על מקש התפריט כדי להוסיף" />
```

Exactly two lines. The title states the absence without apology; the subtitle names the one key that resolves it, in second-person masculine imperative. No illustration, no button (there is nothing to focus here), no exclamation mark.
